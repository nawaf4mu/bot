Privacy Policy for NA Security Bot

1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how NA Security Bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, and handles data. By adding the Bot to your server, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data Collection
The Bot collects the following minimal information to provide its security services:

User IDs & Guild IDs: To identify users and servers.
User Roles: To manage role persistence (restoring roles for returning members).
Channel IDs: To store the designated logs and honeypot channels.
Softban Statistics: To keep track of the number of users caught in the honeypot trap.
3. How Data is Used
The collected data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

Role Persistence: Saving and restoring roles when members leave and rejoin.
Server Protection: Monitoring for raids, nuking attempts, and NSFW content.
Administrative Logs: Providing server administrators with logs of security actions.
4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored in a secure MongoDB database.
We do not store passwords, private messages (unless they trigger a security filter), or sensitive personal information.
Data is only accessed by the bot's automated systems to perform its functions.
5. Third-Party Sharing
NA Security Bot does not sell, trade, or share any collected data with third-party companies or individuals.

6. Data Deletion
If a server administrator removes the Bot from their server, the bot will no longer collect data from that guild. To request the complete deletion of your data, please contact the bot owner.

7. Compliance with Discord Terms
The Bot operates in full compliance with the Discord Developer Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Contact
For any questions regarding this policy, please contact the bot owner via Discord.
